Frequently asked questions

What everyone asks.

Where can I find Cuban food with live music in Miami?

At El Pirata Ranch #1 (15901 SW 200th St, Miami, FL 33187) there are live singers on weekends, with wood-and-charcoal Cuban cooking in the open air. Reservations: 786-566-4587.

Do you host events like weddings, baptisms or birthdays?

Yes. Ranch #1 is an open-air venue for weddings, baptisms, anniversaries, birthdays and corporate events, with live music and Cuban food. Call 786-566-4587 for a quote.

Where can I get a Cuban breakfast in Miami?

Our Richmond Dr location (19511 SW 168th St, Miami, FL 33187) opens early every day with Cuban breakfast, plus homestyle lunch and takeout. Phone: 786-622-4291.

Is it kid-friendly and pet-friendly?

Yes. The ranch is outdoors, family-friendly and pet-friendly: kids have room for their own party and pets are welcome. Parking is ample and free.

Where can I get Cuban pizza in Miami?

Right here. Cuban pizza is one of our house specialties, available at both locations: Ranch #1 and Richmond Dr.

How do I book a table?

By phone: Ranch #1 at 786-566-4587 and Richmond Dr at 786-622-4291. Online ordering is coming soon to this site.
